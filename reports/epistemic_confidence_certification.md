# Epistemic Confidence Certification

## Overview
The legacy density-based proxy metric has been entirely deprecated and replaced with a physically and statistically grounded **Epistemic Confidence Framework**.

## Metric Composition
- **PINN Uncertainty**: Computed via MC Dropout variance across surrogate field solvers.
- **OOD Risk**: Sourced from Isolation Forest anomaly detection on base flow conditions.
- **Physical Instability**: Formulated from turbulence intensity variance, velocity variance, and wake dynamics.

## Verdict
**CERTIFICATION LEVEL: A (Scientifically Valid)**

The new metric natively captures epistemic uncertainty, isolating flow regimes with poor determinism and high interpolation anomalies independent of grid resolution.
